1. English Grammar

Parts of Speech

Words are grouped according to the work they do in a sentence. The main parts of speech are noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction and interjection.

  • Noun: names a person, place, thing or idea. Example: teacher, Lahore, book.
  • Pronoun: is used instead of a noun. Example: he, she, they.
  • Verb: shows an action or state. Example: read, write, is.
  • Adjective: describes a noun. Example: a clever student.

Simple Present Tense

We use the simple present for habits, routines and facts. With he, she or it, the main verb usually takes -s or -es.

Examples: I study every day. She reads a book. The sun rises in the east.

Practice: Fill in the blank: Ali ___ (go) to school every day.

2. Mathematics

Order of Operations

When an expression contains different operations, solve brackets first, then powers, then multiplication or division from left to right, and finally addition or subtraction from left to right.

Example: 8 + 3 × 2 = 8 + 6 = 14.

Practice: Solve: 18 − 4 × 3.

Percentage

Percent means “per hundred”. To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

Example: 20% of 150 = (20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30.

Practice: Find 15% of 200.

3. Physics

Speed, Distance and Time

Speed tells us how much distance an object covers in a unit of time.

Formula: Speed = Distance ÷ Time.

Example: A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Its average speed is 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

Practice: A cyclist covers 30 km in 3 hours. Find the average speed.

SI Units

The SI unit of length is metre (m), mass is kilogram (kg), and time is second (s). Standard units help people compare measurements accurately.

4. Chemistry

Elements, Compounds and Mixtures

  • Element: a pure substance made of one type of atom, such as oxygen or iron.
  • Compound: a substance formed when elements chemically combine in a fixed ratio, such as water (H₂O).
  • Mixture: two or more substances physically combined, such as sand and salt.

Practice: Is air an element, a compound or a mixture? Explain your answer.

5. Urdu

اسم، فعل اور حرف

  • اسم: کسی شخص، جگہ یا چیز کے نام کو اسم کہتے ہیں۔ مثال: علی، لاہور، کتاب۔
  • فعل: کام یا حالت ظاہر کرنے والے لفظ کو فعل کہتے ہیں۔ مثال: پڑھنا، لکھنا، کھیلنا۔
  • حرف: وہ لفظ جو دوسرے الفاظ کے ساتھ مل کر معنی واضح کرے۔ مثال: سے، تک، پر۔

مشق: جملے “علی کتاب پڑھتا ہے” میں اسم اور فعل کی نشاندہی کریں۔

6. Computer Science

Hardware and Software

Hardware means the physical parts of a computer that we can touch, such as the keyboard, monitor and mouse.

Software means the programs and instructions that tell a computer what to do, such as a web browser or word processor.

Practice: Is a keyboard hardware or software? Is a web browser hardware or software?

  6. Give one example of computer hardware.
